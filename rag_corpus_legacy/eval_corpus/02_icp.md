# Ideal customer profile

This profile is fictional and written for demonstration.

## Best-fit customers
Mid-market and enterprise companies in healthcare, financial services and retail, with 500 to 5,000 employees. They already collect data but report from scattered spreadsheets and ageing databases. The buyer is usually a Chief Data Officer, VP of Analytics or Head of Operations, with a named budget for data modernisation.

## Buying signals
Recent cloud migration announcements, new data leadership hires, regulatory reporting pressure, and public job postings for data engineers.

## Poor-fit customers
Very small companies with no data team, and organisations that only want staff augmentation with no outcome ownership.

## Competitive positioning
Large consultancies offer breadth but are slower and costlier. Offshore body shops are cheap but rarely own outcomes. Kai's Company competes on small senior squads, fixed-scope pricing and a clear handover. We do not claim to match the scale of the largest firms.
