# Case studies

All clients and figures below are invented for demonstration.

## Aurora Labs: preference data for alignment
Aurora Labs is a fictional AI lab that needed preference data for a new language model. Kai's Company ran an RLHF program with calibrated reviewers. Reviewer agreement rose from 71 percent to 88 percent over three months, and the lab shipped its model update on schedule.

## Meridian Robotics: egocentric task data
Meridian Robotics is a fictional robotics company that needed egocentric video of household tasks. Kai's Company supplied capture kits and task scripts and delivered 12,000 annotated clips in ten weeks. The client reported a measurable gain in its manipulation policy after retraining.

## Northgate Pay: evaluation for a support assistant
Northgate Pay is a fictional fintech company that wanted to compare vendors for a support assistant. Kai's Company designed a blind human evaluation with a scoring rubric. The study ran in four weeks as a fixed-scope project, and the client used the protocol for later releases.

## Lessons
Agreeing quality targets before production avoided most rework. A sampled pilot built trust and led to a managed service in two of the three accounts.
