# Ideal customer profile

This profile is fictional and written for demonstration. Customer segment describes who the client is.

Kai's Company serves six customer segments: AI Labs, Autonomous Vehicle Companies, Enterprises, Fintech Companies, Product and Technology Companies, and Robotics and Physical AI Companies.

## Best-fit customers
Our best-fit customers are companies with active model training or evaluation programs and at least 200 employees. They have a named budget for external data work and a buyer such as a Head of ML Platform, VP of AI Research or Chief AI Officer. Reference markets are the US, Europe, LATAM and APAC.

## AI Labs
AI labs train foundation and multimodal models and need preference data, evaluation and multimodal datasets. The buyer is usually a VP of AI Research. Strong signals are new model releases, hiring for data operations and public research on alignment.

## Autonomous Vehicle Companies
Autonomous vehicle companies need labeled sensor and video data and scenario evaluation. The buyer is usually a head of perception or data operations. Strong signals are new testing permits and fleet expansion.

## Enterprises
Enterprises in energy, healthcare and insurance build internal AI systems and need evaluation and managed programs. The buyer is often a VP of AI Transformation. Strong signals are published AI strategy and new data leadership hires.

## Fintech Companies
Fintech companies need human review for risk models, fraud detection and assistants. The buyer is often a Head of Risk Models. Compliance requirements are strict and data usually has to stay in region.

## Product and Technology Companies
Product and technology companies need annotation and labeling at volume for features such as search, recommendation and moderation. The buyer is usually a Head of ML Platform.

## Robotics and Physical AI Companies
Robotics and physical AI companies need egocentric video and task data for training manipulation and navigation policies. Strong signals are funding rounds and new robot platform announcements.

## Poor-fit customers
Very small teams without a model program are a poor fit. Organisations that want only low-cost labeling with no quality requirements are also a poor fit.
