# Case study: Northwind Health

Northwind Health is a fictional regional healthcare network. Figures are invented for demonstration.

## The challenge
Clinic managers built weekly reports by hand from five separate systems. Reports arrived four days late and the numbers rarely matched.

## What Kai's Company did
We delivered a cloud data warehouse with automated nightly ingestion, defined twelve shared KPIs, and launched an executive dashboard. A pilot assistant let managers ask questions about the reporting policy handbook and receive answers with page citations.

## Results after six months
Reporting time fell from four days to under one hour. Report disputes dropped by roughly 70 percent. The project ran as a fixed-scope engagement of ten weeks, followed by a monthly managed service.

## Lessons
Agreeing KPI definitions in week one avoided most late rework. The client's own analysts were trained to maintain the pipelines.
