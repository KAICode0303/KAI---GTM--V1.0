# Case Study — First-Person Household Activity Data for Embodied AI

## Engagement type
Collection of first-person household activity recordings for an unnamed AI data program. The source connects the dataset to embodied-AI and activity-understanding workflows; it does not state that a robot was deployed or trained successfully.

## Challenge
Capture physical household tasks from a consistent first-person viewpoint while maintaining device, resolution, privacy and submission quality requirements.

## Scope
A short activity-recording engagement using compatible consumer mobile devices, specified wide-angle lens configurations and high-definition capture. The source mentions several hours of activity and privacy-compliant quality control.

## Approach
Coordinate contributors; validate devices and camera configurations; provide task-specific recording instructions; review submissions using quality and privacy checks.

## Reported outcomes
Broader first-person household activity coverage, more consistent activity recordings and privacy/QC controls in the collection workflow. The dataset supported embodied-AI and activity-understanding use cases. The source does not quantify model-performance gains.

## GTM relevance
Relevant to humanoid robotics, home robotics, embodied-AI research and robot-learning teams that need egocentric observations and real-world human task data.
