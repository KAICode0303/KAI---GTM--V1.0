# Kai's Company — Capabilities

## Positioning
Kai's Company is a platform-led human intelligence and AI execution partner. It combines human expertise, data operations, technology, and quality governance to support AI development and validation. Its offering covers data creation, model support, testing and specialist human evaluation. This is a representative project knowledge base, not a statement about a separately incorporated business.

## Core delivery model
Four complementary layers support engagements: (1) human contributors and domain expertise, (2) technology, AI/ML automation and digital workflows, (3) global language, market and industry expertise, and (4) security, compliance and quality guardrails. The service lifecycle includes matching requirements to qualified contributors, executing data/model work, performing quality control and delivering validated assets.

## AI model evaluation and human feedback
Model evaluation and benchmarking; human evaluation and preference judgments; prompt/response evaluation; AI agent and computer-use-agent testing; model validation; adversarial testing, red teaming and trust-and-safety assessments; specialized evaluators and subject-matter experts. Support may include rubric-based quality review, prompt evaluation and expert assessment of AI outputs.

## Data collection, annotation and enrichment
Collection of real-world and multimodal data; image and video annotation; text and document data; labeling and enrichment; dataset QA and validation; data preparation for AI training and evaluation. Training-data operations can combine collection, annotation, review and quality checks.

## Speech, language and cultural expertise
Speech and audio collection; transcription; transcription audits and quality review; multilingual annotation; language and locale metadata; cultural and linguistic evaluation; native-language and specialist contributors.

## Robotics and physical AI data
Real-world physical-task data collection; first-person or egocentric activity video; human demonstrations; sensor and LiDAR/geospatial data; environment data; annotation and physical-world validation support. These are relevant capabilities for robotics and embodied-AI workflows; an individual case study does not establish a particular robot deployment.

## Other capability areas
Image, video and synthetic-data workflows; field data and mapping; UX and application testing; domain-specific expert review; surveys and market intelligence; technical and specialist workforce mobilization.

## Delivery and limitations
A typical engagement involves requirements definition, qualified contributor selection, execution, optimization and quality validation, and delivery. No standard hourly rate, fixed delivery duration, guaranteed future revenue or five-year company forecast is stated in this knowledge base. Do not invent these details.
