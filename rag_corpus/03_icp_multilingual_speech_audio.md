# ICP — Multilingual Speech and Audio AI

## Source basis and scope
This ICP is an adaptation synthesized from the supplied capability deck and the speech/audio/transcription engagements in the case-study deck. It is **not** a separately supplied or independently validated original ICP. Customer priorities and buying personas below are proposed GTM targeting guidance, not confirmed customer relationships.

## Ideal customer
Organizations building or improving speech recognition, speech-to-text, conversational or voice AI, multilingual audio systems, and audio-enabled foundation models that need language-qualified human contributors, audio datasets, transcription, annotation, and linguistic quality assurance.

## Priority customer segments (proposed)
- Tier 1: Speech-to-text and automatic speech recognition (ASR) providers; conversational/voice AI companies; multilingual speech model developers.
- Tier 1: Audio data and evaluation platforms supporting speech model training, annotation, review and validation.
- Tier 2: Multimodal foundation-model teams with substantial speech/audio requirements; enterprise voice-assistant and contact-center AI programs.
- Tier 2: Regional-language technology and localization-focused AI teams.
These tiers are a GTM recommendation inferred from the supplied capabilities, not priority rankings stated in an original speech ICP.

## Customer problems and Kai's Company service mapping
- Need representative multilingual and regional speech → native/fluent contributor recruitment, locale-based audio collection, speaker diversity and language coverage.
- Need training-ready speech/text pairs → recording guidelines, audio/transcript alignment, structured metadata and transcription.
- Need reliable transcription quality → human review, correction, terminology checks and transcription audits.
- Need language, locale and accent metadata → language-specialist review, accent/locale verification and issue categorization.
- Need bilingual and code-switching coverage → multilingual transcription, bilingual script validation and QA.
- Need distributed scale → coordinated language-specific staffing, submission standards and quality controls.

## Relevant evidence from supplied projects
- Bilingual Malay-English conversational speech collection with aligned audio and structured transcription metadata.
- Multilingual audio collection across several language groups and regional English locales.
- Transcription and annotation operations across a reported multi-language program.
- French and Italian transcription audits addressing accuracy and language/locale/accent metadata.
- Medical speech-transcription review requiring terminology checks and privacy-aware handling.
These examples establish documented service experience; they do not establish relationships with any named customers or guaranteed performance outcomes.

## Buying personas (proposed)
- Executive sponsors: CTO, VP AI/ML, Head of Speech AI, Head of Conversational AI.
- Technical buyers: Head of ASR, Speech ML Lead, Director of AI Data, Head of Data Operations, Speech Data Engineering Lead.
- Evaluation/quality owners: Head of Speech Evaluation, Language Quality Lead, Transcription QA Lead, Localization QA Lead.
- Commercial: procurement, vendor management and AI operations.
The roles are suggested targeting personas inferred from the projects; the uploaded materials do not explicitly validate this buyer list.

## Qualification signals
Active speech-model development, launches into new languages/locales, rising transcription QA workload, need for code-switching data, demand for native-language reviewers, or gaps in audio collection/annotation capacity.

## Evidence boundaries
No hourly rates, named clients, verified customer contracts, model-accuracy gains or revenue forecasts are provided. Do not invent them.
