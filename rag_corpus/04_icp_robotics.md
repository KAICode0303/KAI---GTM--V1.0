# ICP — Robotics and Physical AI

## Ideal customer
Companies building, training, evaluating or deploying robotics, autonomous systems and embodied AI that need real-world data, human demonstrations, annotation, sensor datasets, physical testing and specialized robotics expertise.

## Priority segments
Tier 1: humanoid robotics, home/consumer robotics, autonomous vehicles and ADAS, robot learning/embodied AI. Tier 2: warehouse/logistics robotics, industrial robotics, drones/autonomous aerial systems, medical robotics and agricultural robotics. Tier 3: robotics research labs.

## Customer problems and service mapping
- Need real-world rather than simulation-only evidence → physical-task, egocentric, multimodal and sensor data collection.
- Need training examples → human demonstrations, annotation, enrichment and feedback.
- Need validation in physical environments → physical AI evaluation, testing, human validation and QA.
- Need domain-specific execution → robotics contributors, technical/domain experts and distributed field operations.

## Buying personas
Decision makers: CTO, Chief AI Officer, VP AI/ML, VP Robotics, VP Engineering, Head of Robotics, Head of AI/ML. Technical buyers: Director of Robotics, Director of AI/ML, Director of Data, Robotics Engineering Manager, ML Engineering Manager, Head of Data Operations, Head of AI Evaluation. Commercial: Head of Procurement, Strategic Sourcing, Vendor Management, Supplier Management, Operations Director and Program Manager.

## Qualification signals
Real-world pilots, human demonstration requirements, sensor or egocentric data needs, physical task evaluation and robotics contributor requirements. A venture-backed robotics business with approximately 100–2,000 employees is an illustrative ICP, not a claim about any client.
