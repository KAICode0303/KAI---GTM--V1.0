# Service catalog

Kai's Company is a fictional AI data services firm used only for this demonstration. Every service below is delivered by trained specialists with quality review.

Kai's Company offers eight services: AI Training Data Collection, Annotation and Labeling, RLHF and Human-in-the-Loop, Model Evaluation and Benchmarking, Multimodal Data Services, Robotics and Egocentric Data, Managed AI Programs and AI Consulting.

## AI Training Data Collection
We collect custom text, speech, image and video datasets that match a client's specification. Collection programs run with consented contributors and documented provenance. A typical program delivers a sampled pilot batch within two weeks and scales after sign-off.

## Annotation and Labeling
We label text, images, audio and video for classification, detection, segmentation and transcription. Every labeling program uses written guidelines, gold-standard checks and a second-pass review. Typical customers are product and technology companies that need labeled data at volume.

## RLHF and Human-in-the-Loop
We run preference ranking, response rewriting and red-team style review for language model alignment. Reviewers are screened and calibrated against expert answers before they join a program. AI labs and fintech teams use this service most.

## Model Evaluation and Benchmarking
We design evaluation sets, run blind human evaluations and report model quality against agreed benchmarks. Deliverables include a scoring rubric, an error analysis and a repeatable evaluation protocol. Enterprises use this service to compare vendors and track regressions.

## Multimodal Data Services
We create and curate datasets that combine text, image, audio and video, including aligned captions and cross-modal labels. Programs include a data specification workshop and a pilot before full production. AI labs training multimodal models are the main buyers.

## Robotics and Egocentric Data
We capture egocentric video and sensor data of real tasks for robotics and physical AI teams. Capture kits, task scripts and privacy controls are supplied by Kai's Company. Autonomous vehicle and robotics companies buy this service.

## Managed AI Programs
We run end-to-end data programs as a managed service with a dedicated program lead, weekly reporting and agreed quality targets. The client sets goals and Kai's Company owns day-to-day delivery. This is our largest service by pipeline value.

## AI Consulting
We advise on data strategy, evaluation design and vendor selection for AI teams. Engagements are short and fixed in scope, such as a four-week data readiness review. Consulting often leads to a managed program.
