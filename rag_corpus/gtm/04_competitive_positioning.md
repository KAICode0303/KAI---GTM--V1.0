# Competitive positioning

This positioning is fictional and written for demonstration.

## Crowd labeling platforms
Crowd platforms are fast and cheap but give limited control over quality. Kai's Company screens and calibrates reviewers and adds a second-pass review. We do not claim to match the raw volume of the largest platforms.

## Offshore vendors
Offshore vendors offer low hourly rates but often lack program management and quality reporting. Kai's Company supplies a dedicated program lead and weekly reporting.

## In-house teams
In-house teams give full control but are slow to scale and hard to staff for specialist work. Kai's Company can extend an in-house team with a time-and-materials engagement or take over delivery as a managed service.

## Large outsourcing firms
Large outsourcing firms offer breadth but move slowly and rarely specialise in AI data. Kai's Company competes on specialist reviewers, faster pilots and clear quality targets.

## Common objections
When a prospect says price is too high, we compare cost per accepted item rather than cost per hour. When a prospect worries about quality, we offer a sampled pilot with gold-standard scoring. When a prospect worries about data security, we describe consented collection and in-region handling.
