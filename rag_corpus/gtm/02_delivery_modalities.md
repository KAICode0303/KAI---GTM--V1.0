# Delivery modalities

Modality describes how Kai's Company delivers work. It is separate from the customer segment, which describes who the client is.

Kai's Company delivers in three modalities: fixed-scope project, managed service and time-and-materials team.

## Fixed-scope project
A fixed-scope project has an agreed deliverable, schedule and price. It suits well-defined work such as a one-off dataset or an evaluation study. Change requests are priced separately.

## Managed service
A managed service is a recurring program where Kai's Company owns delivery against quality and throughput targets. It suits ongoing needs such as continuous labeling or evaluation. A dedicated program lead reports weekly.

## Time-and-materials team
A time-and-materials team extends the client's own team with named specialists billed by time. It suits exploratory work where the scope will change. The client directs the work day to day.

## Choosing a modality
We recommend a fixed-scope project when the scope is stable, a managed service when volume is steady and a time-and-materials team when the scope is uncertain. Many accounts start with a fixed-scope pilot and move to a managed service.
