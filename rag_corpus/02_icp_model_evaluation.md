# ICP — AI Model Development and Evaluation

## Ideal customer
Organizations actively developing, fine-tuning, testing, benchmarking or evaluating AI models that require scalable human evaluation, specialized expertise, evaluation datasets, validation and continuous model improvement.

## Priority segments
Tier 1: generative AI/LLM developers, foundation model companies, AI agent companies, enterprise AI platforms, conversational and voice AI, multimodal AI, AI safety and trust-and-safety providers. Tier 2: AI search/recommendation, computer vision, vertical AI and AI research labs. Tier 3: AI infrastructure/MLOps when evaluation is part of the requirement.

## Customer problems and service mapping
- Continuous evaluation across tasks, languages and domains → model evaluation, benchmarking, prompt evaluation, QA and validation.
- Need for specialist judgments → domain experts, technical experts, language specialists and specialized evaluators.
- Need for evaluation data → collection, annotation, enrichment, preference data and QA.
- Edge cases and safety weaknesses → agentic AI testing, red teaming, trust and safety, human feedback and model validation.

## Buying personas
Decision makers: CTO, Chief AI Officer, VP AI, VP ML, VP Engineering, Head of AI, Head of ML and Head of AI Research. Evaluation owners: Head of AI Evaluation, Head of Model Quality, Evaluation Lead, Model Validation Lead. Technical buyers: Director of AI/ML/Data Science, AI/ML Engineering Manager, ML Research Manager, Data Science Lead. Safety owners: Head of AI Safety, Responsible AI Lead, Trust and Safety Lead. Commercial stakeholders: procurement, sourcing, vendor management and AI operations.

## Qualification signals
Active proprietary model work, frequent releases, benchmarking needs, recurring human evaluation, multilingual/domain evaluation and safety testing. An illustrative target account might be a venture-backed AI company with approximately 100–2,000 employees; this is an ICP example, not a verified customer statistic.
